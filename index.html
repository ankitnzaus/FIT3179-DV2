AUSTRALIA'S FOOD SYSTEM

From Farm to Fork

How Australia's food system begins, moves and reaches our tables

Australia produces a diverse range of food across vastly different landscapes. This visualisation explores where food is produced, what different states specialise in, how the system connects to markets, and the pressures that shape what Australians ultimately eat.

01

Where Australia's food begins

Australia's horticultural production is unevenly distributed across the country, with a small number of states accounting for much of the value generated by fruit, vegetables, nuts and wine grapes.

02

What Australia produces

Australia’s food production varies significantly across states. Wheat production is concentrated in major broadacre regions, while horticultural industries show distinct state-level specialisations.
